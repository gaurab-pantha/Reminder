Privacy Policy for Reminder

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Hi! I'm the developer of Reminder, a simple Chrome extension made to help people keep track of their tasks and reminders.

What information does Reminder use?

Reminder may store the tasks and reminder details you add, such as titles, dates and times, so the extension can work properly.

Where is my data stored?

If Reminder uses only Chrome's local storage, your reminder information stays in your browser and is not sent to my server.

Do I share your information?

I do not sell your personal information. If the extension works entirely locally, your reminder details are not sent to me or shared with third parties.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete your reminders inside the extension. You may also remove the extension's stored data through Chrome.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's features or data practices change, I may update this privacy policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, you can contact me through the contact email listed on the Chrome Web Store.
